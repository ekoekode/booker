Jonas Hale wrote the letter three times and burned it twice.

*Dear Tamsin*, it began, and he could get no further than that. Tamsin Reed had left Ember Keep in the autumn, and the silence she left behind had grown heavier than any argument.

He thought about what he might say. That he was sorry. That the archive had swallowed him. That he had found something in the old ledgers, a missing page that might be a map, and that he wished she were here to read it with him.

The candle guttered. Outside, the wind worried at the shutters.

At last he folded the third attempt, unsigned, and slid it into the back of the ledger where no one would look. Some words were safer waiting.
