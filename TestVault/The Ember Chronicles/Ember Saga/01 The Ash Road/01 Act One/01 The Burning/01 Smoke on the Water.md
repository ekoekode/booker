The Harbor of Sel burned all night, and Mara Voss watched it from the black water with her fingers locked around a broken oar.

Every warehouse on the quay had become a lantern. The smoke rolled low across the waves, and the sound of it was like a great animal breathing.

"You will not live until morning if you stay here," said a voice behind her.

Mara turned. A man stood in a flat boat that had no business being so steady in such a swell. This was Kest, though she did not yet know his name. He held out one hand, and the scar along her left hand ached in answer.

"Who sets fire to a whole city?" she asked.

"Someone who wanted it quiet afterward." Kest smiled without warmth. "Come. I know a way out of Sel that the fire has not found."

She looked once more at the burning quay. Then she took his hand, and the harbor of her childhood went dark behind her.

* * *

By dawn the ferry had reached open water, and Mara had not spoken once. *Remember this*, she told herself. *Remember every light of it.*
