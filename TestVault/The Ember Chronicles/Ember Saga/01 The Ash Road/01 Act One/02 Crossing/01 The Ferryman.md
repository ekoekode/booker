The river was wide and grey, and the ferryman had been waiting for Mara Voss longer than she would ever guess.

"Fare," said Kest, not looking up from his pole.

"I have no coin."

"I did not ask for coin." He pushed off from the bank. The water made no sound beneath the hull. "Tell me instead what you are running toward."

Mara thought about it as the far shore slid closer through the mist. "My brother," she said at last. "Or the man who took him."

"Both walk the Ash Road," said Kest. "One ahead of you and one behind."

"Which is which?"

"That," said the ferryman, and for once he smiled, "is the part that costs extra."

She laughed despite herself, a dry short sound. The boat touched the stones, and Mara stepped out onto the road of ash.
