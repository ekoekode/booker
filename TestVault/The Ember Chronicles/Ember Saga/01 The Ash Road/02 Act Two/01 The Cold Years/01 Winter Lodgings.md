Jonas Hale had not expected Ember Keep to be so cold, nor the archive to be so full of other people's grief.

He set his lamp on the long table and unrolled the ledger of winter lodgings. Mara Voss sat across from him, wrapped in a coat that did not suit her, turning a cup of tea she did not drink.

"You have read all of this?" she asked.

"Most of it," Jonas said. "The keep took in forty refugees the year Sel fell. Not one of them gave the same account of the fire."

"People remember what they can bear."

"Perhaps." He tapped the page. "But someone wrote *all lodgings closed by order* here, and the hand is not the steward's."

Mara leaned forward. The lamp threw her scar into relief, a pale ridge across her knuckles.

"Then we know where to start," she said.
