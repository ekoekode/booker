Ember Keep stood on its cliff like a held breath, and Kest led Mara Voss through its gates without a word to the guards.

In the great hall a fire burned in a hearth tall enough to stand in. Mara stared at it a long while before she spoke.

"I will find whoever lit Sel," she said. "I swear it on this fire, and on my name."

"Oaths are heavy things," Kest said. "They have a way of outliving the people who make them."

"Then let it outlive me."

An old steward cleared his throat from the shadows. He had been a clerk in Sel, he said, and he had seen the ferries leave. On the last boat there had been a young man with dark braids tied just as Mara's were.

*Her brother.* The word went through her like cold iron.

"He was alive?" she whispered.

"He was when he left," said the steward. "I cannot promise more than that."

Mara pressed her burned hand flat against the hearthstone and felt the heat, and was glad of it.
