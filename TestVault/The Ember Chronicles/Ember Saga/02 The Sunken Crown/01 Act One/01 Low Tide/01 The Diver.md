Before there was a Harbor of Sel, there was only a bay, and Kest was the first to dive into it.

The water was cold and green. He went down with a stone in his fist, past reefs that no one had yet named, until the silt rose around him like smoke.

*Something is down here*, he thought. *Something that will want to be found.*

At the bottom lay a ring of pale stone, and in the ring's center a circle of gold that had not yet learned it was a crown.

He did not take it. Not then. He rose slowly, lungs burning, and decided to tell no one for ten thousand years.

* * *

Above him the tide turned, and the bay began, very slowly, to be a harbor.
