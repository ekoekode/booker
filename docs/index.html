Why Booker exists

The time is short, and the work matters.

Jesus is coming back soon. Artificial intelligence is changing how people think and write, and the world is in upheaval. I read these things in the light of Scripture, and they seem to me to point to the season at hand.

Booker is a tool for anyone to write a book, and especially for the community of faith who hope in Jesus: the best tools to study Scripture freely and carefully, and to write down what they find in sermons, devotionals, commentaries, teaching and books. It runs offline on your own computer, with nothing to sign up for.

Whatever you write, may it point people to Christ.

Study

Walk the ancient paths with the fathers of the faith.

Read, compare and cite without leaving the page you are writing. Download what you want from one window.

  • Bible reader with the Church Fathers' commentary beside every verse
  • Hebrew and Greek word study with Strong's numbers, line by line
  • Several translations, and verses inserted with a footnote and reference in your own style
  • Dictionary and thesaurus, plus search by meaning that runs on your own computer
Write

And write it down.

Your sermon series, study, commentary or novel is right there: today's words, your streak, a sprint timer, and every part one click away.

  • Write, Plan, Codex and Export cards that scroll inside, with a filter that searches titles and text
  • Typeset writing mode: first-line indents, paragraph and dialogue spacing, even in the editor
  • Drag sections between chapters and parts, and name every level your own way
  • People and places are noticed as you write and added to the note for you
Plan

See the whole story at once.

Plotlines like a subway map, a timeline you can zoom, and a map of every place your characters have been.

Plotlines

A real world map that works offline, or your own drawn worlds with their own grids.

Get started

Three steps to your first chapter.

You need Obsidian 1.5 or newer. One download covers Mac, Windows and Linux.

Download

Get the Booker zip from the releases page and unzip it. You will find a folder called booker.

Copy it in

Put that folder in your vault/.obsidian/plugins/, then turn on Booker under Community plugins.

Start writing

Run Booker: New project, pick your kind of book, and follow the checklist on the Today card.

🔒

Your writing stays yours.

No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Booker only goes online when you press a Download button or check for updates, and everything else, including search by meaning and the dictionary, runs on your own computer. On phones and tablets Booker works too, without the local search engine.
