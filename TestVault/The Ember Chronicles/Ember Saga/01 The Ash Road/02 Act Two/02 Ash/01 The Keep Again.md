Mara Voss climbed the steps of Ember Keep for the second time in her long life and found them smaller than she remembered.

"It has not changed," Jonas Hale said, breathing hard behind her.

"It has," said Kest from the top of the stair. "You have."

The great hearth was cold now. Mara laid her palm on the stone where she had once sworn her oath, and felt nothing at all. That was almost worse than the heat.

"Someone stole the map from this hall," Jonas said. "The archive records the theft but not the thief."

"Then the thief knew the oath," Mara said slowly. "Knew what I would one day come back to find."

Kest said nothing. He only looked at the ashes, and for a moment he seemed very old indeed.
