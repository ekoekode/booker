They unrolled the map on a flat stone beside the Ash Road, weighting its corners with their boots.

Jonas Hale bent close. The ink was brown with age, and the coastline was one he knew: the curve of a harbor, a broken quay, a tower now gone.

"This is Sel," he said. "But look at the margin."

Mara Voss looked. Someone had drawn a small crown there, half submerged in waves, and beneath it a single line of writing.

"*Where the fire began, the water keeps.*" She read it twice. "What does that mean?"

Kest had gone very still. "It means," he said, "that your oath was never about the fire."

**The map was a door, and it opened onto the sea.**

Jonas rolled it up again with careful hands. None of them spoke for a long time.
